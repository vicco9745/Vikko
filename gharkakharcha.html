🧾 Ghar Ka Kharcha Kya Hota Hai?

Ghar ka kharcha matlab — woh saari rakam jo ek mahine mein ghar chalane par kharch hoti hai. Chahe woh khane peene ki cheezein hon, bills hon, bachhon ki padhai hon ya koi emergency — sab milake ghar ka kharcha banta hai!

Ghar ke main kharche hote hain:

🍚
Khana Peena
Roz ka khaana, ration
💡
Bills
Bijli, paani, gas
🏫
Padhai
Bachhon ki fees
🏥
Emergency
Dawai, doctor

✅ Yaad rakho: In sabko milake jo total kharcha hota hai — wahi ghar ka kharcha hai! Jab tak yeh pata nahi hoga — control karna impossible hai.

💰 Ghar Ka Kharcha Kaise Kam Kare?

Yeh 5 tarike apnao — ghar ka kharcha zaroor kam hoga!

📦 1. Bulk Mein Samaan Kharido
Daal, chawal, aata — ek saath zyada quantity mein kharido. Per kg sasta padta hai! Monthly ₹300-500 bachte hain.

🏷️ 2. Discount Aur Offer Dekho
Sale mein zaruri cheezein kharido. Off-season mein kapde lene par 40-50% discount milta hai! Lekin sirf zaroori cheezein — faltu nahi!

💡 3. Electricity Bachao
LED bulb lagao — AC 24°C par rakho — sote waqt sab band karo. Bijli bill ₹300-800 kam ho sakta hai!

🎯 4. Daily Budget Fix Karo
Roz ka ek fixed budget rakho. Jaise — "Aaj ₹200 se zyada nahi kharachhunga." Yeh ek rule bahut kaam aata hai!

🏦 5. EMI Kam Rakho
EMI income ke 30% se zyada nahi honi chahiye. Agar zyada hai — naya loan mat lo! EMI zyada = saving zero.

🧾 Kul Kharcha Kya Hota Hai?

Kul Kharcha matlab — ek mahine mein aapke ghar par har cheez par jo bhi paisa kharch hota hai — woh sab milake jo total banta hai.

Yeh sirf bade kharche nahi hain — balki chhote chhote roz ke kharche bhi is mein shamil hote hain! Chai, bus ticket, sabzi — yeh sab milke ek bada amount banta hai.

🏠
Ghar Ke Kharche
Kiraya, bijli, paani, gas
🍚
Khaane Ke Kharche
Ration, sabzi, bahar khaana
🚌
Yatra Ke Kharche
Bus, petrol, auto
👤
Personal Kharche
Kapde, dawai, mobile

⚠️ Zaroori Baat: Bahut log sirf kiraya aur ration count karte hain. Lekin chhote kharche milake sabse bada portion bana dete hain! Isliye kul kharcha mein sab kuch count karna zaroori hai.

🔢 Kul Kharcha Kaise Calculate Kare?

Kul kharcha calculate karna bahut aasaan hai! Bas yeh 4 steps follow karo:

✍️ Step 1: Har Kharcha Likho

Ek paper ya mobile notes mein is mahine ke sab kharche likho. Koi bhi mat bholo — chahe ₹10 ki chai ho ya ₹5,000 ka EMI.

Tip: Roz ke kharche roz likho — mahine ke aakhir mein poori list ready hogi!

✍️ Step 2: Category Mein Baanto

Sab kharche alag alag category mein rakho:

🏠 Ghar: ₹_______
🍚 Khaana: ₹_______
🚌 Yatra: ₹_______
👤 Personal: ₹_______
🏫 Bachche: ₹_______
🏦 EMI / Loan: ₹_______
🎉 Entertainment: ₹_______

Isse pata chalega kahan zyada kharcha ho raha hai!

✍️ Step 3: Sab Jodo

Ab har category ka total nikalo aur phir sab categories ka total jodo.

Kul Kharcha = Sab Categories Ka Total

✍️ Step 4: Income Se Compare Karo

Ab dekho:

💰 Kul Income: ₹_______
💸 Kul Kharcha: ₹_______
🏦 Bachat: ₹_______ (Income – Kharcha)

✅ Agar income zyada hai kharche se — acha hai!
❌ Agar kharcha zyada hai income se — kharcha kam karna hoga!

💡 Kul Kharcha Kam Karne Ke Tarike

Yeh 8 tarike apnaao — kul kharcha zaroor kam hoga!

🛒 1. Ration Bulk Mein Kharido
Mahine mein ek baar sab le lo. Per kg sasta padta hai aur extra kharidna band hoga. Bachat: ₹300-500/mahina

🍽️ 2. Bahar Khaana Kam Karo
Hafte mein ek baar se zyada mat jao. Ghar ka khaana 5 guna sasta hai! Bachat: ₹1,000-3,000/mahina

💡 3. Bijli Bachao
LED bulb lagao, AC 24°C par rakho, sote waqt sab band karo. Bachat: ₹200-600/mahina

🚌 4. Public Transport Use Karo
Jahan ho sake bus aur metro use karo. Petrol ka kharcha bahut ghata sakte ho! Bachat: ₹300-1,000/mahina

📱 5. Subscriptions Band Karo
Jo apps use nahi karte unka subscription band karo. OTT platforms, gym membership, apps. Bachat: ₹500-1,500/mahina

⏰ 6. 24 Ghante Ka Niyam Apnaao
Koi bhi cheez kharidne se pehle 24 ghante ruko. 70% baar tum nahi kharidoge! Bachat: ₹500-2,000/mahina

💳 7. Cash Use Karo
UPI se 20-30% zyada kharcha hota hai. Cash dene mein dard lagta hai — isliye automatically kam kharcha! Bachat: ₹500-2,000/mahina

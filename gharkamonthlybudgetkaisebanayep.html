⚠️ Zaroori Baat: Sirf guaranteed income likho! Uncertain ya irregular amounts mat likho. Budget hamesha sure income par base hona chahiye.

📌 Step 2: Sare Kharche List Karo

Ab har mahine hone wala har ek kharcha likho! Inhe do groups mein baanto — Fixed aur Variable.

🔒 Fixed Kharche
(Har mahine same)

🏠 Kiraya / Home Loan EMI
🏫 Bachon Ki School Fees
📱 Mobile Recharge
🏦 Loan EMIs
💡 Bijli Bill

Inhe kam karna mushkil hai
📊 Variable Kharche
(Har mahine badalte hain)

🛒 Ration aur Sabzi
🍕 Bahar Khaana
👕 Kapde
🎉 Entertainment
🚗 Petrol aur Travel

Inhe kam kiya ja sakta hai!

✅ Smart Approach: Fixed kharche accept karo — woh jaisi hain waisi hain. Poori energy variable kharche control karne par lagao — waheen saving hoti hai!

📌 Step 3: 50-30-20 Rule Lagao

Ghar ka monthly budget banane ka sabse easy aur popular formula hai 50-30-20 Rule!

50 — 30 — 20 Rule Samjho

🏠 50% → Zarooraten (Needs)
Kiraya + Ration + Bijli + Dawai + Fees
Jo bina nahi chalta
🎉 30% → Chahaten (Wants)
Bahar khaana + Shopping + Manoranjan
Jo accha lagta hai lekin zaroori nahi
💰 20% → Bachat (Savings)
Emergency Fund + Investment + Future Goals
Jo aapka kal banayega

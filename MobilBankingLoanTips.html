1. FD Kya Hoti Hai — Simple Samajhna

💰 Fixed Deposit (FD) = Apna paisa bank mein ek fixed time ke liye lock karo — guaranteed interest milta hai. Market risk zero, returns fixed. India ka sabse popular safe investment — ₹1000 se shuru kar sakte ho, 7 days se 10 years tak.

FD mein jo rate aapko promise hoti hai woh poore tenure mein change nahi hoti — chahe RBI rate badhe ya ghate. Yahi iska sabse bada fayda hai. DICGC ke through ₹5 lakh tak ka insurance bhi milta hai — aapka paisa completely safe hai.

✅ FD Ke Fayde

Guaranteed returns, market risk zero, flexible tenure (7 days se 10 years), DICGC insurance ₹5 lakh, loan le sakte ho FD par, premature withdrawal possible, senior citizens ko extra 0.25-0.5% milta hai.

2. App Se FD Kaise Khole — Step by Step

  • 1

    Bank App Open Karo

    YONO SBI, MyHDFC, iMobile Pay, Axis Mobile — apni bank ki app open karo aur login karo.

  • 2

    Investments / FD Section Dhundho

    "Investments", "Fixed Deposit" ya "Deposits" section mein jaao — usually bottom menu ya home screen par hota hai.

  • 3

    "Open FD" Select Karo

    "Create FD" ya "Open Fixed Deposit" button tap karo.

  • 4

    Amount Enter Karo

    Deposit amount type karo (minimum ₹1000, most banks mein). Source account select karo.

  • 5

    Tenure Select Karo

    Duration choose karo — days/months/years. Interest rate automatically calculate ho kar dikhega.

  • 6

    Interest Payout Choose Karo

    Monthly/Quarterly payout ya Cumulative (maturity par sab) — apni zarurat ke hisab se select karo.

  • 7

    OTP Se Confirm Karo

    Terms review karo, OTP enter karo — FD instantly create ho jaati hai. Receipt download karo.

3. FD Types Samajhna

📈

Cumulative FD (Reinvestment)

Interest maturity tak reinvest hota rehta hai — compound interest ka fayda milta hai. Long-term savings ke liye best. Maturity par principal + full interest ek saath milta hai.

Best for Long Term
💸

Non-Cumulative FD (Payout)

Interest monthly/quarterly/annually mein milta rehta hai — regular income chahiye to yeh choose karo. Senior citizens aur retirees ke liye popular. Principal maturity par milta hai.

Regular Income
🔒

Tax Saver FD

5 year lock-in period — premature withdrawal allowed nahi. Section 80C ke under ₹1.5 lakh tak tax deduction milti hai. Interest par tax lagta hai lekin principal par tax benefit. Maximum ₹1.5 lakh per year.

Tax Saving
⚡

Short Term FD (7 days - 1 year)

Emergency fund ya short-term goals ke liye. Rates slightly kam hoti hain long-term se. Liquid — kaam aane par break karo easily. Bank app se instantly open aur close.

Flexible

💡 SFB vs Large Bank — Kya Choose Kare

Small Finance Banks (SFB) mein rates zyada hain lekin risk bhi thoda zyada. DICGC ke under ₹5 lakh tak insured hai — isliye ₹5 lakh tak SFB mein safe hai. Large banks mein rates kam hain lekin trust aur stability zyada. Risk diversify karo — dono mein invest karo.

✅ Simple Rule

Agar aapke paas lump sum paisa hai (bonus, gift, savings) — FD choose karo. Agar aap monthly chhota chhota invest karna chahte ho — RD choose karo. Dono safe hain, dono guaranteed returns dete hain.

6. Premature FD Withdrawal — Kab Aur Kaise

⚠️ Premature Withdrawal Penalty Lagti Hai

FD maturity se pehle todne par usually 0.5-1% penalty applicable rate par lagti hai. Matlab agar aapko 7% milni thi, to 6-6.5% milegi. Emergency mein FD todne se pehle FD pe loan lo — yeh smarter option hai.

FD Par Loan — Better Option!

  • FD value ka 80-90% loan mil sakta hai instantly
  • FD intact rehti hai aur interest milta rehta hai
  • Loan interest sirf FD rate + 1-2% hota hai (personal loan se kaafi kam)
  • Bank app se instant FD loan apply karo — 2 minute mein amount milta hai
  • Loan repay karte hi FD free ho jaati hai — koi penalty nahi

Premature Withdrawal Karne Ke Steps (App Se)

  • 1

    Bank App > FD Section

    Apni existing FDs list mein jaao.

  • 2

    FD Select Karo

    Jis FD ko break karna hai use tap karo.

  • 3

    "Premature Closure" Select Karo

    Option available hoga — penalty amount bhi dikhega.

  • 4

    Confirm Karo

    OTP ya MPIN se confirm karo — amount bank account mein credit ho jaayega.

7. FD Par Tax — TDS Guide

  • FD interest aapki income ke hisab se taxable hai — income tax slab rate se
  • ₹40,000 se zyada FD interest per year par bank 10% TDS kaatta hai
  • Senior citizens ke liye TDS limit ₹50,000 per year hai
  • Form 15G (non-senior) ya Form 15H (senior) bank mein dene par TDS nahi katega — agar income taxable nahi hai
  • ITR file karte waqt FD interest "Income from Other Sources" mein declare karo
  • Multiple banks mein FD hai to sab ka combined interest calculate karo
💡 TDS Bachane Ka Tarika

Agar aapki total income basic exemption limit se neeche hai to Form 15G/15H apni bank mein har saal April mein submit karo. Online bhi submit kar sakte ho bank app se. TDS kat gaya hai to ITR file karke refund le sakte ho.

8. FD Smart Tips

1

FD Ladder Banao

Ek saath sab paisa ek FD mein mat daalo. ₹5 lakh ko 5 parts mein baat do — alag alag tenure ki FDs banao (1, 2, 3, 4, 5 years). Liquidity maintain rahegi aur har saal ek FD mature hogi.

Expert Strategy
2

Rate Comparison Karo Pehle

FD kholne se pehle 2-3 banks ki rates compare karo. Same tenure mein 0.5% difference se ₹1 lakh par 5 years mein ₹2,500+ extra milta hai. BankBazaar ya bank websites se compare karo.

Extra Returns
3

Senior Citizen Bonus Use Karo

Agar ghar mein senior citizen hain (60+) to unke naam par FD kholो — 0.25-0.5% extra rate milta hai. Joint FD bhi ho sakti hai — first holder senior citizen hona chahiye extra rate ke liye.

Extra 0.5%
4

Auto-Renewal Set Karo

FD create karte waqt "Auto-Renewal" option enable karo. Maturity par automatically same tenure mein renew ho jaayegi — prevailing rate par. Paisa idle nahi rahega.

Set & Forget
5

Nominee Add Karo

FD open karte waqt hamesha nominee add karo. Emergency mein ya untoward situation mein family ko FD access karna aasaan ho jaayega bina legal hassle ke.

Important

10. FD FAQ

Mobile banking se FD kaise khole? ›
Bank app open karo > Investments/FD section > Open FD > Amount enter karo > Tenure select karo > Interest payout choose karo > OTP se confirm karo. 2 minute mein done. Minimum ₹1000 se shuru kar sakte ho.
Best FD interest rate 2026 mein kaunsa bank de raha hai? ›
Best rates: Unity SFB 9.5%, Suryoday SFB 9.1% (senior 9.6%). Private banks: HDFC 7.4%, ICICI 7.25%. PSU: SBI 7%. SFB mein zyada rate hai — ₹5 lakh tak DICGC insured hain, safe hai.
FD se pehle paisa nikal sakte hain? ›
Haan, premature withdrawal possible hai — 0.5-1% penalty lagti hai applicable rate par. Better option: FD par loan lo (80-90% value) — FD intact rehti hai, interest milta rehta hai, aur loan rate bhi kam hota hai.
FD aur RD mein kya choose kare? ›
Lump sum amount hai (bonus/gift/savings) → FD. Monthly chhota invest karna hai → RD. FD mein rate thoda zyada hoti hai. Dono safe hain, dono app se instantly open ho sakte hain.
FD par tax kaise lagta hai? ›
FD interest income tax slab rate se taxable hai. ₹40,000+ annual FD interest par bank 10% TDS kaatta hai. Form 15G/15H submit karo agar income taxable nahi hai. ITR mein "Income from Other Sources" declare karo.
FD mein paisa safe hai kya? ›
Haan — DICGC (Deposit Insurance) ke under ₹5 lakh tak completely insured hai har bank mein. Government backed scheme hai. SFB mein bhi same insurance milta hai. ₹5 lakh se zyada invest karna ho to multiple banks mein split karo.

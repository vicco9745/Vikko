📊 The Formula to Calculate Monthly Interest

To calculate monthly interest use this simple formula:

🔢 Monthly Interest Formula

Monthly Interest = (Principal × Rate) ÷ 12 ÷ 100

Where:
📌 Principal = The amount on which interest is charged
📌 Rate = Annual interest rate in %
📌 ÷ 12 = To get 1 month out of 12 months

✅ Example: On ₹1,00,000 at 12% annual interest rate:
Monthly Interest = (1,00,000 × 12) ÷ 12 ÷ 100
= 12,00,000 ÷ 12 ÷ 100
= 1,00,000 ÷ 100
= ₹1,000 every month!

📌 Simple Interest — Monthly Calculation

Simple Interest is the interest that is charged only on the principal amount — not on the previous interest.

🔢 Simple Interest Formula:

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100
Where:
P = Principal Amount
R = Annual Interest Rate %
T = Time in Years

Monthly SI = SI ÷ (T × 12)

📝 Example 1 — Simple Interest on ₹50,000

Given:
Principal (P) = ₹50,000
Interest Rate (R) = 10% per year
Time (T) = 1 year

Calculation:
SI = (50,000 × 10 × 1) ÷ 100
SI = 5,00,000 ÷ 100
SI = ₹5,000 (for 1 year)

Monthly Interest = ₹5,000 ÷ 12 = ₹416.67 every month!

📝 Example 2 — Simple Interest on ₹1,00,000

Given:
Principal (P) = ₹1,00,000
Interest Rate (R) = 8% per year
Time (T) = 2 years

Calculation:
SI = (1,00,000 × 8 × 2) ÷ 100
SI = 16,00,000 ÷ 100
SI = ₹16,000 (for 2 years)

Monthly Interest = ₹16,000 ÷ 24 = ₹666.67 every month!

📌 Compound Interest — Monthly Calculation

Compound Interest is the interest that is charged on the principal amount as well as on the previous interest. It is also called "interest on interest"!

⚠️ Important: Compound Interest is always higher than Simple Interest! In loans it works against you — but in investments it works in your favour!
🔢 Compound Interest Formula:

A = P × (1 + R/100)^T
Where:
A = Total Amount
P = Principal Amount
R = Annual Interest Rate %
T = Time in Years

CI = A – P
Monthly CI = CI ÷ (T × 12)

📝 Example — Compound Interest on ₹1,00,000

Given:
Principal (P) = ₹1,00,000
Interest Rate (R) = 10% per year
Time (T) = 2 years

Calculation:
A = 1,00,000 × (1 + 10/100)²
A = 1,00,000 × (1.10)²
A = 1,00,000 × 1.21
A = ₹1,21,000

CI = 1,21,000 – 1,00,000 = ₹21,000

Monthly Interest = ₹21,000 ÷ 24 = ₹875 every month!

📊 Comparison: Simple Interest = ₹666.67/month Compound Interest = ₹875/month
Compound Interest is ₹208 more!

🏦 How to Calculate Monthly Loan Interest

In a loan the EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) contains both Principal and Interest combined.

🔢 Monthly Loan Interest Formula:

Monthly Interest = Outstanding Loan × Monthly Rate
Where:
Monthly Rate = Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100

📝 Example — Monthly Interest on Home Loan

Given:
Loan Amount = ₹10,00,000
Annual Interest Rate = 8.5%

Calculation:
Monthly Rate = 8.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.00708
Monthly Interest = 10,00,000 × 0.00708
Monthly Interest = ₹7,083 every month!

This means ₹7,083 of your EMI is just interest!

📈 How to Calculate Monthly FD Interest

In an FD (Fixed Deposit) you receive interest. Calculating monthly interest is very simple!

🔢 FD Monthly Interest Formula:

Monthly Interest = (FD Amount × Annual Rate) ÷ 12 ÷ 100

📝 Example — Monthly Interest on FD

Given:
FD Amount = ₹2,00,000
Annual Rate = 7%

Calculation:
Monthly Interest = (2,00,000 × 7) ÷ 12 ÷ 100
= 14,00,000 ÷ 12 ÷ 100
= 1,16,667 ÷ 100
= ₹1,167 every month!

💡 Important Tips for Monthly Interest Calculation

✅ Tip 1: Always check the rate as Annual
Banks and finance companies mostly give annual rates. Always divide by 12 to get the monthly figure.

✅ Tip 2: Compare monthly rate with annual
If someone says 2% monthly — that means 24% annually! That is very high — think carefully!

✅ Tip 3: Calculate interest before taking any loan
Before taking any loan — calculate the full interest first! Then decide.

✅ Tip 4: Choose Compound Interest for FD
For FD choose the quarterly compound option — you will receive more interest!

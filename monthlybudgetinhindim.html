🧾 What is a Monthly Budget?

A Monthly Budget is a written plan that tells you how you will use your money every single month. It covers:

💰

Income
How much comes in?
Salary + Side income
💸

Expenses
How much goes out?
All monthly costs
🏦

Savings
How much stays?
Your future

✅ The Formula: Income – Savings – Expenses = 0
This means — every single rupee has a job. No rupee goes anywhere without a plan!

📌 How to Make a Monthly Budget — Step by Step

✍️ Step 1: Write Down Your Total Income

Write down everything that comes in every month:

💼 Salary: ₹___________
📱 Part time work: ₹___________
🏠 Rent received (if any): ₹___________
💰 Any other income: ₹___________

Total Income = ₹___________

⚠️ Write only guaranteed income!

✍️ Step 2: List All Your Expenses

Write down every monthly expense — divided into two groups:

Fixed Expenses Variable Expenses
🏠 Rent / EMI 🛒 Groceries and vegetables
🏫 Children's school fees 🍕 Eating out
📱 Mobile recharge 👕 Clothing
💡 Electricity bill 🚗 Petrol and travel
🏦 Loan EMIs 🎉 Entertainment

✍️ Step 3: Take Out Savings First

"Income – Savings = Expenses"

As soon as salary arrives — take out savings first!
Run all expenses from what remains.

Example:
Income = ₹15,000
Savings (20%) = ₹3,000 → Separate account
Remaining ₹12,000 → All expenses ✅

✍️ Step 4: Set a Limit for Every Category

Set a maximum limit for every expense:

🛒 Groceries: no more than ₹3,000
💡 Electricity: no more than ₹800
🍕 Eating out: no more than ₹1,000
📱 Mobile: no more than ₹399

When the limit arrives — stop spending! ✅

✍️ Step 5: Track Every Day

Budget is made — now write down every expense daily!

Whether it is ₹5 or ₹5,000 — note everything.
In the PaisaTrack app just 1 minute a day gets everything tracked. Completely free!

✍️ Step 6: Review at End of Every Month

Sit down for 30 minutes and check:

✅ Where did you stay within budget?
❌ Where did you go over budget?
💰 How much did you save?

The next month will always be better!

💰 The 50-30-20 Rule — Easiest Monthly Budget Formula

The most popular and easiest formula for making a monthly budget is the 50-30-20 Rule!

50 — 30 — 20 Rule

🏠 50% — Needs
Rent + Groceries + Electricity + Medicine + Fees
What you cannot live without — Needs
🎉 30% — Wants
Eating out + Shopping + Entertainment
What feels good — Wants
💰 20% — Savings
Emergency Fund + Investment + Future Goals
What will build your tomorrow — Savings

🎯 Tips to Follow Your Monthly Budget

💵 Tip 1: Use Cash
Take out a fixed amount of cash at the start of every week. Cash runs out = spending stops! People spend 20-30% more with UPI than with cash.

🤝 Tip 2: Involve the Whole Family
Tell everyone in the family about the budget. When everyone knows — everyone controls!

🔄 Tip 3: Set Up Auto Transfer
As soon as salary arrives — savings automatically go to a separate account. What you cannot see — you cannot spend!

⏰ Tip 4: The 24 Hour Rule
Before buying anything big — wait 24 hours. 70% chance you will not buy it!

⚠️ The Cardinal Rule of Cashback Credit Cards

Always pay 100% of your credit card bill before the due date — every single month. If you carry a balance at 36–42% annual interest, you will pay ₹3,600–4,200 in interest on a ₹10,000 balance — wiping out every rupee of cashback earned and then some. Cashback on credit cards is only profitable if the card balance is cleared fully every month, zero exceptions.

🛒 Online Shopping Cashback — Category-Wise Guide

1

Amazon Shopping — Layer 3 Cashbacks

The optimal Amazon cashback stack: (1) Go through CashKaro first — earn 3–8% portal cashback on Amazon orders. (2) Apply Amazon coupon codes from CouponDunia (extra 5–15% off on select products). (3) Pay with HDFC Millennia or ICICI Amazon Pay credit card for additional 2–5% cashback. This triple stack earns 10–18% total on Amazon purchases. On ₹10,000 monthly Amazon spending, this generates ₹1,000–1,800 monthly cashback — ₹12,000–21,600 annually — from your existing shopping.

💰 ₹1,000–1,800/month on ₹10K Amazon spend
2

Flipkart Shopping — Big Billion Days Strategy

Flipkart's Big Billion Days (October) and End of Season Sale (January/July) offer the highest cashback events of the year. Strategy: (1) Shortlist products in advance and add to wishlist. (2) On sale day, access via CashKaro for portal cashback. (3) Use Axis Flipkart Credit Card for 5% extra cashback. (4) Check for HDFC/SBI bank exclusive additional discount codes. During Big Billion Days, stacking all four can earn 20–35% effective cashback on purchases. Plan all major annual purchases around these dates.

💰 20–35% stacked cashback during sales
3

Grocery Shopping Cashback — Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart

Grocery delivery apps offer significant cashback with the right strategy. Blinkit (Google-backed) frequently runs 10–20% off on specific items via Google Pay. Zepto has monthly membership discounts. Swiggy Instamart offers up to 60% off with Swiggy One membership. Stack with: Axis ACE card (4% on Swiggy payments) + PhonePe cashback offers. On ₹5,000 monthly grocery delivery spending, this earns ₹400–800 back. Plus: compare prices across Blinkit, Zepto and DMart Ready before ordering — prices vary by up to 15%.

💰 ₹300–800/month on grocery delivery
4

Fashion Shopping — Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa Cashback

Fashion apps have the highest portal cashback rates — CashKaro pays 8–15% on Myntra and up to 20% on Ajio. Strategy: (1) CashKaro portal + any active coupon code. (2) Pay with HDFC Millennia (5% on Myntra). (3) Shop during End of Reason Sale (June/December) for maximum 50–80% product discounts stacked with your cashback. Never buy fashion at full price — nearly every purchase can be timed to a sale. Use Myntra's Insider program for free early access to sales and additional loyalty benefits.

💰 8–20% portal cashback on fashion

🏆 20 Proven Cashback Tricks for Maximum Earnings

💳 Credit Card Tricks (1–6)

7

Match Card to Your Biggest Spending Category

The biggest cashback mistake is using a generic card for everything. If your top spending is Amazon → use ICICI Amazon Pay (5%). If it's Swiggy/Zomato → use Axis ACE (4%). If it's general online shopping → use SBI Cashback Card (5% all online). Calculate where 70% of your spending goes and use the card that rewards that category highest. Switching to the right card can triple your cashback rate on the same spending with zero behavioral change.

💰 2x–3x more cashback instantly
8

Use Bank's SmartBuy / Exclusive Portals for Extra Rewards

HDFC SmartBuy, SBI Card exclusive merchant offers, ICICI iMobile deals and Axis Bank partner merchant list — every major bank runs an exclusive shopping portal where card members earn 5–10X rewards on top of normal cashback. Check these portals before every purchase — booking flights through HDFC SmartBuy instead of directly on airline website, for example, earns 10X reward points worth an extra 2–3% cashback. Takes 60 seconds to check — often worth hundreds of rupees on large purchases.

💰 5–10X rewards on portal purchases
9

Request Credit Limit Increase to Lower Utilization

A higher credit limit without increasing spending improves your credit utilization ratio (great for CIBIL) and also qualifies you for higher cashback caps on many cards that have monthly maximums. HDFC Millennia's ₹1,000/month cashback cap is reached at ₹20,000 spending at 5%. With a higher limit and spending, some premium cards have higher caps. Call your bank annually for a limit review — most banks approve it for customers with good payment history.

💰 Better CIBIL + higher cap
10

Redeem Reward Points Before They Expire — Every Quarter

Indians lose thousands of rupees in expired reward points annually. Most credit card reward points expire in 2–3 years with no automatic reminder. Set a quarterly calendar reminder to check and redeem points from every card. Best redemption value: statement credit (direct bill reduction) or Amazon/Flipkart gift vouchers — always better value than merchandise redemption. A 1,000-point balance worth ₹250 left to expire is ₹250 in free money lost forever.

💰 Saves ₹500–2,000 annually
11

Time Large Purchases for Statement Cycle Start

Make large purchases (appliance, laptop, furniture) on day 1–5 of your credit card billing cycle. This gives you maximum interest-free credit — up to 50 days — while your money stays in a high-interest savings account or FD earning returns. On a ₹50,000 purchase with 50 interest-free days, your money earning 7% in IDFC First bank earns approximately ₹480 extra. Small amount individually, but consistent over 12 months adds ₹4,000–6,000 annually from optimal timing alone.

💰 Interest-free float value
12

Split Spending Across 2 Cards for Category Maximization

Use Card A for online spending (SBI Cashback 5% online) and Card B for offline/dining (Axis ACE 4% on Swiggy). This two-card strategy ensures you always earn the maximum possible cashback for every transaction type without leaving money on the table. Managing two cards requires discipline — both bills must be paid in full monthly — but the extra 2–3% on each category adds ₹300–800 more monthly cashback on the same total spending.

💰 Extra ₹300–800/month

Plan all annual major purchases for these windows.

Common Cashback Mistakes That Cost You Money

  • ❌

    Carrying credit card balance to earn cashback: Earning 2% cashback while paying 36% interest on carried balance is a net loss of 34%. Cashback is only free money when you pay 100% of your credit card bill every month without exception.

  • ❌

    Buying things you don't need to "earn cashback": Spending ₹5,000 on unnecessary purchases to earn ₹250 cashback is losing ₹4,750. Cashback should be earned on planned, needed purchases — never used as a justification to spend.

  • ❌

    Not checking cashback portal BEFORE shopping: Going directly to Amazon or Flipkart instead of via CashKaro means losing 3–15% portal cashback every time — often ₹50–500 per purchase for zero extra effort.

  • ❌

    Letting reward points and cashback expire: Credit card reward points, CashKaro balance, Google Pay offers and app wallets have expiry dates. Untracked cashback that expires is money earned and then lost. Set quarterly reminders to review and redeem all cashback balances.

  • ❌

    Using wrong card for each category: Using a 1% cashback card where a 5% card exists costs ₹400 in missed cashback on ₹10,000 spending — every single month. Spend 10 minutes identifying the optimal card for your top 3 spending categories.

  • ❌

    Not stacking cashback sources: Using only one cashback source (just credit card OR just portal) when all three can be stacked reduces cashback earnings by 60–80%. The 5-second habit of always using portal + coupon + card earns 3–5x more on the same purchase.

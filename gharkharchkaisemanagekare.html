📌 Tarika 1: Sabse Pehle Income Aur Expense Samjho

Ghar kharch manage karne ka pehla step hai — poora hisaab samajhna! Jab tak aapko pata nahi ki kitna aa raha hai aur kitna ja raha hai — tab tak manage karna impossible hai.

💰 Income:
— Salary: ₹15,000
— Side Income: ₹2,000
— Kul Income = ₹17,000

💸 Monthly Kharche:
— Kiraya: ₹5,000
— Ration: ₹3,000
— Bill: ₹1,500

💰 Tarika 3: 50-30-20 Rule Apnaao

Yeh duniya ka sabse famous aur aasan budget formula hai! Ek baar samajh lo — phir zindagi bhar kaam aayega.

50-30-20 Rule Kya Hai?

🏠 50% → Zaroorat (Needs)
Kiraya, ration, bijli, dawai, fees
Jo bina nahi chalta
🎉 30% → Chahhat (Wants)
Bahar khaana, shopping, entertainment
Jo accha lagta hai
💰 20% → Bachat (Savings)
Emergency fund, investment, future
Jo aapka kal banayega

Example — ₹17,000 Income par:

Hissa % Rakam Kahan Jaayega
🏠 Zaroorat 50% ₹8,500 Kiraya, ration, bill
🎉 Chahhat 30% ₹5,100 Khaana, shopping, mauज
💰 Bachat 20% ₹3,400 Saving aur investment

🛒 Tarika 4: Mahine Ka Ration Plan Karo

Roz roz kharidna mahanga padta hai! Har baar market jaane par kuch na kuch extra khared lete hain.

✔️ Smart Ration Planning:

📅 Mahine mein ek baar ration kharido
— Daal, chawal, aata, tel — sab ek saath
— Bulk mein sasta padta hai

📝 List banakar jao
— List ke bina market mat jao
— Extra nahi kharidoge

🏷️ Offer aur sale ka faayda lo
— Zaruri cheezein sale mein kharido
— Brand nahi — quality dekho

💰 Result: ₹500-1,500 har mahine bachenge!

💵 Tarika 6: Pehle Bachat Karo — Phir Kharcho!

Yeh Golden Rule hai ghar kharch manage karne ka! Zyaadatar log pehle kharcha karte hain — aur jo bachta hai woh save karte hain. Yahi sabse badi galati hai!

💰 Golden Rule:

"Income – Saving = Expense" ✅

Jaise hi paisa aaye:

✔️ Pehle 10-20% alag account mein daalo
✔️ Baaki se ghar ka poora kharcha chalao

Example:
Income = ₹17,000
Pehle ₹3,400 (20%) → Saving account
Baaki ₹13,600 → Saare kharche ✅

✅ Trick: Auto Transfer set karo — Salary aate hi automatically 20% alag account mein chala jaaye. Jo dikhta nahi — woh kharch nahi hota!

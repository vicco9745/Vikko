🧾 What is a Monthly Budget?

A monthly budget is a written plan where you decide in advance exactly where your money will go this month.

💰
Income
How much comes in?
💸
Expenses
Where does it go?
🏦
Savings
What stays with you?

✅ Simple Formula: Income – Savings – Expenses = 0
This means — every single rupee has a job! No money leaves without a plan!

📌 Step 1: Calculate Your Total Income

The very first step to making a monthly budget is — writing down exactly how much money comes in every month! Do not guess — confirm it in writing.

💰 Write Down All Income Sources:

💼 Main Salary: ₹___________
📱 Side Income / Part Time: ₹___________
🏠 Rental Income (if any): ₹___________
💰 Any Other Income: ₹___________

Total Monthly Income = ₹___________

⚠️ Important: Write only guaranteed income! Do not include uncertain or irregular amounts. Your budget must always be based on sure income.

📌 Step 2: List All Your Expenses

Now write down every single expense you have every month! Divide them into two groups — Fixed and Variable.

🔒 Fixed Expenses
(Same every month)

🏠 Rent / Home Loan EMI
🏫 Children's School Fees
📱 Mobile Recharge
🏦 Loan EMIs
💡 Electricity Bill

Hard to reduce
📊 Variable Expenses
(Change every month)

🛒 Groceries and Vegetables
🍕 Eating Out
👕 Clothing
🎉 Entertainment
🚗 Petrol and Travel

These CAN be reduced!

✅ Smart Approach: Accept fixed expenses — they are what they are. Put all your energy into controlling variable expenses — that is where savings happen!

📌 Step 3: Apply the 50-30-20 Rule

The easiest and most popular formula for making a monthly budget is the 50-30-20 Rule!

Understanding the 50 — 30 — 20 Rule

🏠 50% → Needs (Essentials)
Rent + Groceries + Electricity + Medicine + Fees
What you cannot live without
🎉 30% → Wants (Lifestyle)
Eating out + Shopping + Entertainment
What feels good but is not essential
💰 20% → Savings (Your Future)
Emergency Fund + Investment + Future Goals
What will build your tomorrow

📌 Step 4: Set a Limit for Every Category

Now take your total income and assign a specific limit to every expense category. This is the actual budget!

📊 Sample Monthly Budget — ₹20,000 Income:

🏠 NEEDS (₹10,000 — 50%):
— Rent: ₹5,000
— Groceries: ₹2,500
— Electricity + Water + Gas: ₹1,000
— Mobile + Internet: ₹500
— Medicine: ₹500
— Children's Fees: ₹500

🎉 WANTS (₹6,000 — 30%):
— Eating Out: ₹1,500
— Entertainment: ₹1,000
— Clothing and Shopping: ₹1,500
— Petrol and Travel: ₹1,500
— Personal Care: ₹500

💰 SAVINGS (₹4,000 — 20%):
— Emergency Fund: ₹2,000
— Long Term Investment: ₹2,000

Total = ₹20,000 ✅ — Perfect Balance!

📌 Step 5: Track Every Expense Daily

Making a budget is only half the work. The other half is tracking it every day! Without tracking your budget is just a piece of paper.

📱 How to Track Daily Expenses:

📔 In a Notebook: Always carry it — write every expense immediately

📱 In Mobile Notes: Quick and easy — note every purchase

💻 In PaisaTrack App: The smartest option! Just 1 minute a day — everything gets tracked automatically with graphs and monthly reports!

Rule: Write every expense — even ₹5!

When you track every expense for 30 days — the numbers will genuinely shock you! And once you see where your money is going — smarter choices will follow automatically.

📌 Step 6: Review at the End of Every Month

Take out 30 minutes at the end of every month and sit down with your budget. This monthly review habit is one of the most powerful financial habits you can build!

📋 Ask Yourself These 4 Questions Every Month:

❓
Where did I spend the most?
✅
Where did I stay within budget?
🎯
Did I follow my limits?
💰
How much did I save?

✅ Result: Every monthly review makes the next month's budget even better! This is how continuous improvement happens.

🎯 Tips to Follow Your Monthly Budget

💵 Tip 1: Use Cash
Take out a fixed amount of cash at the start of every week. Cash runs out = spending stops! People spend 20-30% more with UPI than with cash.

🤝 Tip 2: Involve the Whole Family
Tell everyone in the family about the budget. When everyone knows — everyone controls!

🔄 Tip 3: Set Up Auto Transfer
As soon as salary arrives — savings automatically go to a separate account. What you cannot see — you cannot spend!

⏰ Tip 4: The 24 Hour Rule
Before buying anything big — wait 24 hours. 70% chance you will not buy it!

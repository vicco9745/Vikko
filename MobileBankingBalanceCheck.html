Bank Balance Check – 6 Aasaan Tarike

Apna bank balance check karne ke 6 tarike hain: 1) Bank App, 2) Missed Call, 3) SMS, 4) UPI App, 5) USSD (*99#), 6) Net Banking. Sabse fast — Bank App ya UPI App. Bina internet ke — Missed Call ya SMS ya USSD. Sabse easy — Missed Call (sirf call karo, kaat do — SMS mein balance aa jaayega).

1. Bank App Se Balance Check — Sabse Fast

📱
Bank App Method
Internet chahiye — 2 second mein balance
Fastest Free Easy

Bank app open karte hi home screen par balance dikh jaata hai — ya ek tap mein. Yeh sabse convenient tarika hai.

  • 1

    Bank App Open Karo

    YONO SBI / MyHDFC / iMobile Pay / Axis Mobile — apni bank ki app open karo.

  • 2

    Login Karo

    MPIN, fingerprint ya face ID se login karo.

  • 3

    Home Screen Dekho

    Zyatar apps mein home screen par hi balance dikh jaata hai. Nahi dikh raha to "Accounts" ya "Balance" section mein jaao.

  • 4

    Balance Refresh Karo

    Agar latest balance chahiye to "Refresh" ya pull-down gesture karo.

✅ Bank App ke Extra Fayde

Sirf balance nahi — mini statement, last transactions, EMI details, FD balance sab ek jagah milta hai bank app mein.

2. Missed Call Se Balance Check — Bina Internet

📞
Missed Call Method
Internet nahi chahiye — sirf call karo aur kaat do
No Internet Free Easiest

Registered mobile number se missed call do — bank turant SMS mein balance bhej deta hai. Koi charge nahi.

💡 Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Apna registered number se bank ke balance check number par call karo aur 2-3 ring ke baad kaat do (ya call auto disconnect ho jaayegi). Kuch seconds mein SMS mein balance aa jaayega.

3. SMS Se Balance Check — BAL Likho

💬
SMS Method
BAL ya BAL [account last 4 digits] SMS karo
No Internet Free/Nominal
  • 1

    New SMS Kholo

    Phone mein messaging app open karo.

  • 2

    BAL Likho

    Message mein sirf "BAL" likho (capital letters mein).

  • 3

    Bank Number Par Bhejo

    Apni bank ke SMS number par bhejo. SBI: 09223766666, HDFC: 5676712.

  • 4

    Reply Mein Balance Aayega

    Kuch seconds mein bank ka reply SMS aayega jisme account balance hoga.

4. UPI App Se Balance Check — Google Pay / PhonePe

💳
UPI App Method
Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM — kisi bhi UPI app se
Fast Free Easy

Google Pay Se Balance Check

  • 1

    Google Pay Open Karo

    App open karo aur apna account select karo.

  • 2

    Profile Icon Par Tap Karo

    Top right corner mein profile icon par tap karo.

  • 3

    Bank Account Select Karo

    "Bank Account" section mein apna account select karo.

  • 4

    Check Balance Par Tap Karo

    "Check Balance" par tap karo aur UPI PIN enter karo — balance dikh jaayega.

PhonePe Se Balance Check

  • 1

    PhonePe Open Karo

    App open karo.

  • 2

    Profile → Bank Account

    Profile > My Money > Bank Account par jaao.

  • 3

    Check Balance

    "Check Balance" tap karo > UPI PIN enter karo > Balance screen par.

5. USSD *99# Se Balance — Feature Phone Ke Liye Bhi

📟
USSD Method — *99#
Bina internet — basic phone pe bhi kaam karta hai
No Internet Any Phone
  • 1

    *99# Dial Karo

    Phone keypad se *99# dial karo aur call button dabao.

  • 2

    Bank Select Karo

    IFSC code ke pehle 4 letters enter karo — jaise SBI ke liye "SBIN", HDFC ke liye "HDFC".

  • 3

    Account Balance Option

    Menu mein "Account Balance" option select karo (usually option 1 ya 3).

  • 4

    UPI PIN Enter Karo

    UPI PIN enter karo — balance screen par dikh jaayega.

💡 USSD Kab Use Kare

Jab internet nahi ho, ya feature phone use kar rahe ho, ya roaming mein ho. *99# sabhi Indian telecom operators par kaam karta hai — Airtel, Jio, Vi, BSNL sab par.

6. Net Banking Se Balance Check

Net Banking Method

8. Balance Check Smart Tips

💡

Registered Number Se Hi Check Karo

Missed call aur SMS balance check sirf registered mobile number se kaam karta hai — jo bank mein linked hai. Doosre number se karo to response nahi milega.

Important
🔔

Auto Balance Alert Set Karo

Bank app mein "Low Balance Alert" set karo — jab balance ek limit se neeche aaye to notification aayega. Isse overdraft se bachoge aur hamesha aware rahoge.

Free Feature
📊

Monthly Statement Download Karo

Sirf balance nahi — bank app ya net banking se monthly statement bhi download karo. PDF mein milegi — tax filing aur budgeting ke liye kaam aayegi.

Pro Tip
🌐

Multiple Accounts Ek Jagah Dekho

Agar aapke multiple bank accounts hain to PaisaTrack ya Account Aggregator apps use karo — sab accounts ka balance ek jagah dikh jaayega. Bahut convenient hai.

Smart
⚠️ Yeh Galti Mat Karo

Koi bhi website ya app jo "bank balance check karo bina login ke" bolti hai — woh fraud hai. Sirf apni bank ki official app ya website use karo. Kabhi kisi third-party unknown app ko banking permission mat do.

9. Balance Check FAQ

SBI bank balance kaise check kare mobile se? ›
SBI balance check ke 4 tarike: 1) Missed Call: 09223766666. 2) SMS: 09223766666 par BAL bhejo. 3) YONO SBI app open karo. 4) USSD: *99*41# dial karo. Sabse aasaan — missed call.
Bina internet ke balance kaise check kare? ›
3 tarike bina internet ke: 1) Missed Call — bank number par missed call do. 2) SMS — BAL likho bank number par bhejo. 3) USSD — *99# dial karo. Teeno free hain aur kisi bhi phone par kaam karte hain.
UPI se balance check kaise kare? ›
Google Pay: Profile > Bank Account > Check Balance > UPI PIN. PhonePe: Profile > My Money > Bank Account > Check Balance > UPI PIN. BHIM: Account > Check Balance > UPI PIN.
HDFC balance check number kya hai? ›
HDFC missed call: 1800-270-3333. SMS: 5676712 par BAL bhejo. App: MyHDFC open karo. USSD: *99*42# dial karo. Registered number se karo tabhi kaam karega.
Missed call balance check free hai? ›
Haan, bilkul free! Call karo aur 2-3 ring pe kaat do. Bank SMS mein balance bhej deta hai. Koi charge nahi. Sirf registered number se karna zaroori hai.
*99# se balance check kaise kare? ›
*99# dial karo > bank ka IFSC first 4 letters enter karo (SBI=SBIN, HDFC=HDFC) > Account Balance select karo > UPI PIN enter karo > Balance dikhega. Internet nahi chahiye — kisi bhi phone par kaam karta hai.

📌 Step by Step — How to Make Your Budget Plan

✍️ Step 1: Set Your Financial Goals First

Before writing a single number — ask yourself: what do I want to achieve this month?

🎯 Short Term Goals (1-6 months):
— Save ₹10,000 for emergency fund
— Pay off a small loan
— Buy something specific

🎯 Long Term Goals (1-5 years):
— Buy a home
— Children's education fund
— Retirement savings

When you know your goals — your budget has direction!

✍️ Step 2: Calculate Your Total Income

Write down every single source of income:

💼 Main Salary: ₹___________
📱 Side Income: ₹___________
🏠 Rental Income: ₹___________
💰 Other Income: ₹___________

Total Monthly Income = ₹___________

⚠️ Write only guaranteed income — not expected income!

✍️ Step 3: List All Fixed Expenses

Fixed expenses are the ones that stay the same every single month. List them all first.

🏠 Rent / Home Loan EMI: ₹_______
🏫 School Fees: ₹_______
📱 Mobile + Internet: ₹_______
🏦 Loan EMIs: ₹_______
💡 Electricity Bill: ₹_______

Fixed Expenses Total = ₹_______

✍️ Step 4: Plan Your Savings — BEFORE Expenses

This is the most important step that most people skip!

Before planning variable expenses — decide how much you will save.

💰 Emergency Fund contribution: ₹_______
📈 Investment / Long term saving: ₹_______
🎯 Goal-specific saving: ₹_______

Aim for at least 20% of your income!

Remember: Save first — spend what remains!

✍️ Step 5: Plan Variable Expenses with Limits

Now take what is left after fixed expenses and savings — and divide it among variable expenses. Set a LIMIT for each one!

🛒 Groceries: ₹_______ (max limit)
🍕 Eating Out: ₹_______ (max limit)
🚗 Petrol + Travel: ₹_______ (max limit)
👕 Clothing + Shopping: ₹_______ (max limit)
🎉 Entertainment: ₹_______ (max limit)
🏥 Medicine + Emergency: ₹_______ (max limit)

Variable Expenses Total = ₹_______

✍️ Step 6: Check the Balance

Now add everything up and check:

Income = Fixed Expenses + Savings + Variable Expenses?

✅ If yes — your budget plan is balanced!
❌ If expenses are more than income — cut variable expenses until it balances!

Your budget must always balance to zero. Every rupee must have a job!

🎯 The Best Budget Planning Methods

There are several popular methods for making a monthly budget plan. Choose the one that suits you best!

Method 1: The 50-30-20 Rule ⭐ Most Popular

🏠 50% → Needs — Rent, groceries, bills, medicine
🎉 30% → Wants — Eating out, shopping, entertainment
💰 20% → Savings — Emergency fund + Investment

Best for: Beginners and people who want a simple plan. Easy to follow and remember!

Method 2: Zero Based Budget

Income – All Expenses – Savings = Zero

Every single rupee is assigned a job. Nothing is left unplanned.

Best for: People who want complete control and do not mind detailed planning.

Method 3: Envelope Method

Take out cash at the start of the month. Put each category's budget amount in a separate envelope.

— Groceries envelope: ₹3,000
— Entertainment envelope: ₹500
— Eating out envelope: ₹1,000

When the envelope is empty — spending stops!

Best for: People who overspend with UPI and cards.

Method 4: Pay Yourself First

As soon as salary arrives — transfer savings immediately to a separate account. Then spend the rest however you want.

Best for: People who find it hard to save — this method makes saving automatic and effortless!

Here is a complete monthly budget plan for a family earning ₹20,000 per month:

📋 Complete Monthly Budget Plan — ₹20,000

💰 INCOME PLAN
Salary: ₹18,000 + Side Income: ₹2,000 = ₹20,000
💰 SAVINGS PLAN (20% = ₹4,000) — FIRST!

Rent: ₹5,000 | School Fees: ₹1,500 | Mobile: ₹500 | Electricity: ₹1,000

Groceries: ₹2,500 | Travel: ₹1,000 | Eating Out: ₹1,500 | Shopping: ₹1,000 | Entertainment: ₹500 | Medicine: ₹500
✅ BALANCE CHECK
Income ₹20,000 = Savings ₹4,000 + Fixed ₹8,000 + Variable ₹7,000
₹20,000 = ₹20,000 ✅ — Perfectly Balanced!

💪 How to Stick to Your Budget Plan

💵 Use Cash for Variable Expenses
Take out weekly cash for groceries, eating out and shopping. When cash runs out — spending stops! Research shows cash users spend 20-30% less than card users.

🔄 Set Up Auto Savings Transfer
Set a standing instruction with your bank — savings transfer automatically on salary day. What you cannot see you cannot spend!

⏰ Follow the 24 Hour Rule
Before any unplanned purchase — wait 24 hours. 70% of the time you will not buy it!

🤝 Involve Your Whole Family
Share the budget plan with every family member. When everyone knows the plan — everyone helps follow it!

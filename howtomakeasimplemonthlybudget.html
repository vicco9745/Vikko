📌 Step 1: Write Down Your Income

The very first step is simply — how much money comes in every month? Write it down clearly.

💰 Write Your Monthly Income:

💼 Salary: ₹___________
📱 Side Income: ₹___________
🏠 Other Income: ₹___________

Total Income = ₹___________

Tip: Write only the money you are SURE will come in!

📌 Step 2: Write Down Your Expenses

Now write down your expenses. For a simple budget — keep it in just 5 easy categories!

5 Simple Budget Categories

1️⃣ HOME 🏠
Rent + Electricity + Gas + Water + Internet
Everything related to your home
2️⃣ FOOD 🍚
Groceries + Vegetables + Milk + Eating Out
Everything you eat and drink
3️⃣ TRAVEL 🚌
Bus + Petrol + Auto + School Transport
Everything to get from A to B
4️⃣ PERSONAL 👤
Clothing + Medicine + Entertainment + EMIs
Everything else for you and family
5️⃣ SAVINGS 💰
Emergency Fund + Investment + Goals
Your future — always plan this first!

📌 Step 3: Set Savings First — Most Important!

Before you plan any expense — decide how much you will save this month. This is the single most important rule of a simple monthly budget!

💰 Simple Savings Rule:

Save at least 10-20% of your income — FIRST!

As soon as salary arrives → transfer savings to a separate account → then use the rest for expenses.

Example on ₹20,000 income:
Minimum savings = ₹2,000 (10%)
Ideal savings = ₹4,000 (20%)

What you cannot see — you cannot spend! Set up auto transfer so savings leave automatically.

📌 Step 4: Set Limits and Balance Your Budget

Now divide your remaining money (Income minus Savings) among your 4 expense categories.

📊 Simple Formula:

Total Income: ₹_______
Minus Savings: – ₹_______
= Money for Expenses: ₹_______

Now split this among 4 categories:

🏠 Home: ₹_______
🍚 Food: ₹_______
🚌 Travel: ₹_______
👤 Personal: ₹_______

Check: Do all 4 categories add up to "Money for Expenses"?
✅ Yes → Budget is balanced!
❌ No → Reduce one category until it balances.

📌 Step 5: Track and Review

The final step — and the one that makes everything work!

📅 Every Day

Write down every expense — even ₹10 or ₹20.

Takes just 1-2 minutes!

📆 Every Week

🎯 Simple Budget Tips That Actually Work

💰 Tip 1: Save First — Spend What Remains
The moment salary arrives — transfer savings to a different account immediately. You will never miss what you never see!

📝 Tip 2: Write Every Expense — Even Small Ones
That ₹20 chai, that ₹50 snack — they add up to thousands every month! Write everything — get shocked — improve!

🛒 Tip 3: Make a Grocery List Before Shopping
Always go with a list. Never go to the market without one! This one habit can save ₹500-1,000 every month.

🍽️ Tip 4: Eat at Home More Often
Home food costs 5 times less than eating out. Limit eating out to once or twice a week maximum. This saves ₹1,000-3,000 every month!

⏰ Tip 5: Wait 24 Hours Before Any Big Purchase
See something you want to buy? Wait 24 hours. 7 out of 10 times — you will not want it anymore!

1. Transaction Failed – Money Deducted

💸
Transaction Failed But Balance Cut Gaya Urgent
Aapne payment kiya — screen par "Failed" ya "Pending" dikh raha hai lekin bank balance se paise cut ho gaye hain.
✅ Solution Steps
1) Ghabrao mat — yeh common hai aur resolve hota hai.
2) Transaction history mein exact status check karo.
3) 24-48 hours wait karo — auto reversal hota hai.
4) Dobara payment mat karo — double deduction hoga.
5) Nahi aaya to UPI app mein "Raise Dispute" karo.
6) Bank helpline call karo reference number ke saath.
⏰ 48 Hours Ka Rule

Failed transaction ka amount 24-48 hours mein automatically wapas aata hai. Agar 48 hours mein nahi aaya to turant bank helpline call karo aur dispute raise karo. RBI ke rules ke anusaar max 5 business days mein refund milna chahiye.

2. Login Problem – Account Locked

🔐
Mobile Banking Login Nahi Ho Raha Common
3 baar galat MPIN daalne se account lock ho jaata hai. Ya password forgot, ya new device par login nahi ho raha.
✅ Solution Steps
Agar account lock hai:
1) Bank 24×7 helpline call karo — account unlock request.
2) Ya bank branch jaao photo ID ke saath.
3) Usually 24 hours mein unlock hota hai.

Agar password/MPIN forgot:
1) App mein "Forgot MPIN" select karo.
2) Registered mobile number + debit card se verify karo.
3) OTP confirm karo aur naya MPIN set karo.

3. UPI Issues – Pending / Failed

📲
UPI Payment Pending Ya Failed Common
UPI payment kiya — "Pending" dikh raha hai ya "Failed" aaya. Paise balance se cut ho gaye ya nahi pata.
✅ Solution Steps
1) UPI app refresh karo — transaction history dekho.
2) Balance check karo — deducted hua ya nahi.
3) 30 minutes wait karo — server busy ho sakta hai.
4) Deducted hua to 24-48 hours auto reversal wait karo.
5) Nahi aaya to "Raise Dispute" — Google Pay/PhonePe mein available.
6) NPCI helpline: 1800-120-1740.

4. Fraud Report – Emergency Steps

🚨 Fraud Hua Hai — Turant Karo!

48 hours ke andar report karo — refund chances maximum hote hain. Jitna jaldi utna better. Ek second bhi waste mat karo.

  • 1

    Bank Helpline — Turant Call

    Account aur card freeze karne kolo. 24×7 number: SBI 1800-11-2211, HDFC 1800-202-6161, ICICI 1800-200-3344.

  • 2

    Cyber Crime 1930

    National Cyber Crime helpline par call karo — 24×7. Case register hoga aur guidance milegi.

  • 3

    cybercrime.gov.in

    Online complaint file karo — transaction ID, amount, time, screenshots sab ke saath.

  • 4

    Bank Branch — Written Complaint

    Branch mein jaao aur written complaint submit karo. Acknowledgment lena mat bhulo.

  • 5

    Police FIR

    Local police station mein FIR file karo. FIR number note karo — bank aur cyber complaint mein lagega.

5. App Crash / Error Problems

📱
Bank App Crash Ya Error De Raha Hai Quick Fix
App open nahi ho raha, crash ho raha hai, blank screen aa rahi hai ya error message aa raha hai.
✅ Quick Fix Steps
1) App force close karo aur dobara open karo.
2) Phone restart karo.
3) Play Store mein app update check karo — update karo.
4) Phone storage full to nahi? Kuch space free karo.
5) App Settings > Clear Cache karo (data nahi, sirf cache).
6) Uninstall aur fresh install karo.
7) Bank helpline call karo agar kuch bhi kaam na aaye.

6. OTP Nahi Aa Raha – Fix Karo

📞
OTP SMS Nahi Aa Raha Quick Fix
Login ya transaction ke time OTP SMS nahi aa raha — wait kiya lekin kuch nahi aaya.
✅ Solution Steps
1) Mobile network signal check karo — weak signal mein SMS nahi aata.
2) SMS inbox full to nahi? Delete karo purane messages.
3) 2-3 minute wait karo — network delay ho sakta hai.
4) "Resend OTP" button try karo.
5) DND activated hai? Bank numbers DND exempt hote hain — telecom se confirm karo.
6) Registered number sahi hai ya nahi — bank se verify karo.
7) Bank helpline call karo — woh alternative verify kar sakte hain.

7. Account Blocked – Unblock Kaise Kare

🏦
Bank Account Blocked Ho Gaya Urgent
Bank account ya mobile banking blocked hai — transaction nahi ho raha, app mein "Account Blocked" ya "Debit Frozen" aa raha hai.
✅ Solution Steps
Reasons ho sakte hain: Wrong MPIN 3+ baar, KYC expired, suspicious activity, minimum balance.

1) Bank helpline call karo — exact reason poochho.
2) KYC issue hai to Aadhaar/PAN ready karo aur branch jaao.
3) Minimum balance issue hai to amount deposit karo.
4) Suspicious activity hai to bank investigate karega — 24-48 hours.
5) Branch visit karo photo ID ke saath — fastest resolution.

8. Card Block / Replace Guide

🚫

Card Block Karna — Turant

Card chori, gum ya suspicious transaction hone par: Bank app > Debit Card > Block Card. Ya helpline call karo. Ek second mein block hota hai. Naya card 7-10 business days mein milta hai.

Urgent
🔄

Card Replace / Renew Karna

Bank app > Debit Card > Request New Card. Ya branch visit karo. Expired card renew karna bhi isi tarah hota hai. Charge: usually ₹100-200. New card 7-10 days mein courier se aata hai.

Process
📍

Card PIN Bhool Gaya

Bank app > Debit Card > Change/Reset PIN. Registered mobile number par OTP aayega. Naya PIN set karo. Ya nearest ATM par Green PIN service use karo.

Easy Fix

9. Bank Mein Complaint Kaise Kare – Step by Step

Agar bank ne aapki problem 30 days mein resolve nahi ki to escalation karo:

  • 1

    Level 1 — Bank Helpline

    24×7 helpline call karo. Complaint reference number note karo hamesha.

  • 2

    Level 2 — Bank Website Complaint

    Bank ki official website par "Complaint" ya "Grievance" section mein online complaint submit karo.

  • 3

    Level 3 — Branch Manager

    Branch mein jaao aur Branch Manager se personally milо. Written complaint do — acknowledgment lo.

  • 4

    Level 4 — Nodal Officer

    Bank ke Nodal Officer ko email karo — contact details bank website par milti hain.

  • 5

    Level 5 — RBI Ombudsman

    30 days ke baad bhi resolve nahi hua to RBI Banking Ombudsman: bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in. Ya Sachet portal use karo.

✅ Complaint Tips

Hamesha reference number note karo. Sabhi communications ka record rakho. Written complaint mein — date, amount, account number, transaction ID sab include karo. Jitni jaldi complain karo, utna better.

11. Help Desk FAQ

UPI transaction failed but paise cut gaye — kya kare? ›
24-48 hours wait karo — auto reversal hota hai. Nahi aaya to UPI app mein "Raise Dispute" karo ya bank helpline call karo. NPCI: 1800-120-1740. Reference number hamesha note karo.
Mobile banking account locked — kaise kholen? ›
Bank helpline call karo ya branch jaao photo ID ke saath. "Forgot MPIN" option se bhi unlock kar sakte ho — registered mobile + debit card se verify karo. 24 hours mein resolve hota hai.
Bank app crash ho raha hai — kya kare? ›
Steps: 1) Force close + restart. 2) App update karo. 3) Cache clear karo. 4) Uninstall/reinstall. 5) Phone restart. 5 mein se koi ek kaam karega — 95% cases mein.
Bank fraud report kaise kare? ›
Turant: 1) Bank helpline call karo — account freeze. 2) Cyber Crime 1930. 3) cybercrime.gov.in. 4) Bank written complaint. 5) Police FIR. 48 hours ke andar karo — refund chances maximum.
Bank ne complaint resolve nahi ki — kya kare? ›
Escalation path: Helpline > Online complaint > Branch Manager > Nodal Officer > RBI Banking Ombudsman (bankingombudsman.rbi.org.in). 30 days ke baad resolve nahi hua to Ombudsman approach karo — free aur powerful.
OTP nahi aa raha — quick fix kya hai? ›
Quick checklist: Network signal strong hai? SMS inbox full to nahi? 2-3 min wait kiya? Resend try kiya? DND on to nahi? Registered number active hai? Agar sab checked hai to bank helpline call karo.

💡 Ghar Kharch List Kaise Chalaye?

Sirf list banana kaafi nahi hai — use follow bhi karna hai! Yeh 3 kaam karo:

✔️ Roz Update Karo

Din khatam hone se pehle — sab kharche likho.

Raat ko sone se pehle — 10 minute nikalo — aur poore din ka hisaab karo!

Yeh chota kaam bohot bada farak laata hai!

✔️ Har Hafte Check Karo

Sunday ko hafte ka hisaab dekho:

— Kitna kharcha hua?
— Kaunsi category mein zyada gaya?
— Kya kuch faaltu tha?

Jab dikhega — tab hi control hoga!

Jab limit aaye — kharcha rokо! ✅

🔧 Ghar Kharch Kaise Manage Kare?

List banana start kiya — ab manage karna bhi seekho! Yeh 3 kaam sabse zaroori hain:

3️⃣ Saving Zaroor Jodo!

"Pehle Bachao — Phir Kharchao!"

List mein saving ko bhi shamil karo!
Salary aate hi pehle 10-20% alag karo — phir baaki se ghar ka kharcha chalao.

Saving = ₹1,000 bhi sahi hai shuruat ke liye!

💰 Ghar Ka Kharcha Kaise Kam Kare?

Yeh 5 tarike apnao — ghar ka kharcha zaroor kam hoga!

🛒 1. Bulk Mein Samaan Kharido
Daal, chawal, aata — ek saath zyada quantity mein kharido. Per kg sasta padta hai! Monthly ₹300-500 bachte hain.

⚡ 2. Bijli Bachao
LED bulb lagao — AC 24°C par rakho — sote waqt sab band karo. Bijli bill ₹300-800 kam ho sakta hai!

🧾 3. List Ke Anusar Hi Kharidari Karo
Ghar kharch list mein jo likha ho — sirf wahi kharido. Extra nahi! Bina list ke market mat jao.

💳 4. Cash Use Karo
Cash dene mein dard hota hai — isliye automatically kum kharcha hota hai! Hafte ki shuruat mein cash nikalo — jab khatam ho jaye — kharcha band!

📉 5. EMI Kam Rakho
EMI income ke 30% se zyada nahi honi chahiye. Zyada EMI = saving zero! Naya loan sirf zaroorat par lo.
